Nemesis EuRoC 2025

Aurora’s first competition rocket, Nemesis, is a single-stage vehicle, powered by a commercial solid motor, designed to carry a 1 kg payload up to an apogee of 3000 m and to return safely to the ground thanks to a recovery system. Read more about the propulsion system and its solid motor with a non-toxic APCP-based propellant or the recovery system.

The rocket is designed with reliability, ease of maintenance and assembly in mind. The aerostructure ensures stability and integrity, housing the propulsion system, electronics bay, recovery system, and our payload, Aether.

We offer students an exciting, hands-on journey into the world of aerospace engineering by designing, building and testing rockets from the ground up. To operate efficiently, our association is structured into departments, each covering a specific area of expertise.
