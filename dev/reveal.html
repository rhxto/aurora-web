WHO WE ARE

Aurora Rocketry is an Italian non-profit organisation of the University of Bologna based in Forlì. Our team brings together passionate minds from diverse academic backgrounds, creating a truly multidisciplinary environment. This diversity pushes us to think beyond limits, combining knowledge and approaches to achieve ambitious goals and shape future careers in engineering, aerospace and more.

WHAT WE DO

We offer students an exciting, hands-on journey into the world of aerospace engineering by designing, building and testing rockets from the ground up. To operate efficiently, our association is structured into departments, each covering a specific area of expertise.
